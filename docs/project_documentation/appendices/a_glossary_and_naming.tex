\section{Case, Side, and Identity Conventions}

ROS endpoints are lowercase absolute names rooted at \rosname{/room_315}.
Rail side is lowercase \code{left}/\code{right}. Public switches use uppercase
\code{A1--A4}; segments use uppercase A/E/I names. Visual identities are short
\code{L1--L4,R1--R4}; Gazebo/rail entity names are
\code{room315_left_shuttle_1--4} and
\code{room315_right_shuttle_1--4}. Keep identity and entity distinct in APIs.

Naming is shared by the ROS interfaces, rail runtime, visual runtime, and launch
composition domains in Table~\ref{tab:glossary-source-keys}. Review those
owning files together when migrating a public name.
