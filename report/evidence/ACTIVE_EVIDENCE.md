The three pytest suites overlap in a small number of files and their pass
counts must not be summed. The integrated campaign is a positive-case
acceptance matrix, not a reliability estimate or a fault-injection campaign.
The ten-case language result is an interface acceptance set, not a general
language-accuracy or latency benchmark.
